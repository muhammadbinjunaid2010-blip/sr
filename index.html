Install SkillRun on Android

Chrome on your phone or tablet

  1. 1

    Open SkillRun at skillrun.com in Chrome.

  2. 2

    Tap the “3 dots” menu (top right), then tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.

  3. 3

    Tap “Install”. SkillRun opens as its own app — full screen, no browser.

  4. 4

    Find the SkillRun icon on your home screen and start building.

Install SkillRun on iPhone / iPad

Safari on your device

  1. 1

    Open SkillRun at skillrun.com in Safari.

  2. 2

    Tap the Share button (square with an arrow, at the bottom).

  3. 3

    Tap “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.

  4. 4

    SkillRun now runs as its own app on your home screen — full screen, no browser.

Install SkillRun on Windows

Download and install as a desktop app

  1. 1

    Download the installer:

Download for Windows
  1. 2

    Open the downloaded file. Microsoft Defender may say “Windows protected your PC” — this is normal for a new unsigned app. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

  2. 3

    Follow the installer. SkillRun opens as a full desktop app — no browser needed.

Heads-up: the green warning about “harm your PC” is Microsoft’s standard notice for any app that isn’t code-signed yet. It’s safe — just pick More info → Run anyway.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about SkillRun.

Do I need programming experience?

No. SkillRun is designed to start from the beginning.

Is SkillRun free?

Yes. SkillRun is completely free.

What can I learn?

Currently Web Development — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Additional technology paths are planned.

Do I need to install anything?

On your phone, install SkillRun as an app from Chrome or Safari (see the Download section). On Windows, download and run the desktop installer. No browser is used while you learn.

Can I use SkillRun on my phone?

Yes. On Android and iOS, install SkillRun as an app from Chrome or Safari. It runs full screen, just like a native app.

Will SkillRun teach robotics?

That’s part of the longer-term roadmap — electronics, robotics, AI, and connected hardware projects.
